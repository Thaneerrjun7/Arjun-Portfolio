AJ - Personal AI Assistant

A voice assistant that runs on my own Windows laptop. I designed a local streaming voice pipeline (voice activity detection, speech-to-text, a quantised Qwen3-4B on llama.cpp, text-to-speech) that fits a 6 GB laptop GPU and in benchmarks starts speaking 0.83 to 1.37 s after I stop talking. I chose the models by benchmarking 5 local LLM setups on latency and tool-call accuracy (Qwen3-4B: 91.7%, 55 of 60 runs over 20 scripted commands), and cut GPU stalls on a down-clocked laptop GPU from about 300 ms to 30 ms.

SivaSafe

Voice-controlled desktop assistant for elderly users, built solo over two semesters in Python. I designed it around one Gemini session that holds the spoken conversation and calls tools in 19 capability areas, choosing one agent over the proposal's multi-agent design to keep it cheaper and faster. Sign-in uses face recognition (OpenCV YuNet and SFace) with a cosine-similarity match whose threshold I raised from 0.363 to 0.65, a two-blink liveness check, and a spoken password transcribed offline by a Whisper model.
